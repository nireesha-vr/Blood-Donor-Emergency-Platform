How It Works

Simple and efficient process to bridge the gap between donors and emergency patients.

Register as a Donor

Fill out your details, blood type, location, and availability to join our donor directory.

2

Search for Blood

Search available donors by blood group and city to locate nearby volunteers instantly.

3

Respond to Emergency

View real-time emergency requests posted by patients & hospitals and volunteer to donate.

Save a Life

Coordinate directly with the hospital or requester and complete your life-saving blood donation.
