About BloodConnect

BloodConnect is a frontend web platform designed to connect voluntary blood donors directly with patients and hospitals during critical blood emergencies. By leveraging modern client-side storage, BloodConnect simplifies the process of finding compatible donors in real-time.

Our Mission

To eliminate blood shortage delays in medical emergencies by creating a fast, accessible, and transparent donor platform.

How It Helps

Enables patients and hospitals to directly locate nearby verified blood donors or broadcast urgent requests to community helpers instantly.

Why It Matters

A single blood donation can save up to 3 lives. Quick matching between donors and requesters reduces critical waiting time.

Platform Features

Key functionality powering the BloodConnect platform

Donor Registration

Easy-to-use registration form capturing donor contact details, blood group, city, and real-time availability status.

Blood Search

Search and filter donors by blood group and city location instantly without page reloads.

Emergency Requests

Broadcast urgent patient blood requirements with hospital details, required units, and urgency levels.

Donor Matching

Interactive blood compatibility matrix guiding users on donor-recipient compatibility rules.

Responsive Design

Fully optimized for desktop computers, laptops, tablets, and mobile smartphones.

Easy-to-Use Interface

Clean medical aesthetic, intuitive navigation, fast client-side performance, and user feedback toasts.

Why Donate Blood?

Discover the life-saving impact and health benefits of voluntary blood donation.

Save Up To 3 Lives

Whole blood donation can be separated into red cells, plasma, and platelets to help multiple patients.

Free Health Screening

Before donating, your pulse, blood pressure, body temperature, and hemoglobin levels are checked.

Promotes Heart Health

Regular blood donation helps maintain healthy iron levels in the body, supporting cardiovascular health.

Mental Wellbeing
