Blood Type Compatibility Guide

Quick reference for compatible donor and recipient blood types during medical transfusions.

Universal Donor: O-

O Negative (O-) blood can be donated to patients of any blood group (A+, A-, B+, B-, AB+, AB-, O+, O-). Highly requested in trauma emergencies.

Universal Recipient: AB+

Patients with AB Positive (AB+) blood group can safely receive red blood cells from all donor blood groups.

Group A & B Compatibility

Group A can donate to A and AB. Group B can donate to B and AB. Rh-negative types can donate to both Rh-positive and Rh-negative recipients.

Donation Frequency

Healthy individuals can donate whole blood every 90 days (3 months). Always ensure you are well-hydrated and rested before donating.
